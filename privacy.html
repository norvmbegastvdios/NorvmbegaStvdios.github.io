Last updated: November 2026

This is a plain-language starting point, not legal advice. Review it and adjust it to match how your site and assets actually work.

What we collect

This website has no accounts and doesn't use advertising trackers. If you use the contact form, we receive the name, email address and message you submit, and we use them only to reply to you.

How the contact form works

Messages are delivered by Web3Forms, a form service that forwards them to us by email. Their handling of your data is described in their own privacy policy.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard technical data, such as IP addresses, when you visit, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.
