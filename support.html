Support

Stuck? We're happy to help.

Get in touch

Use the contact form and we'll reply as soon as we can. Please include the details below so we can help faster.

When reporting a problem

  • Your Unity version and render pipeline (Built-in, URP or HDRP).
  • The version of our asset you're using. See the changelog.
  • What you expected to happen and what happened instead.
  • Any error messages from the Unity Console, and steps to reproduce the issue.

Before you write

Many questions are answered in the documentation, including the FAQ.
