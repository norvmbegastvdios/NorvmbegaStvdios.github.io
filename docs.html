Install

  1. Get the asset from the Unity Asset Store and add it to your assets.
  2. In Unity, open Window → Package Manager → My Assets.
  3. Select the asset, then click Download and Import.

Requirements: Unity 2021.3 LTS or newer. Works with Built-in, URP and HDRP. (Edit to match your asset.)
